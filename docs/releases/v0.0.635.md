## Summary

This release maps the SuccessFactors workbook columns to real dictionary field names and checks the fill against the customer's own Employee and Foundation workbooks. It also records the closed project items: the signed-in acceptance run, the licensor's grant sign-off and the licence narrowing.

## Features

- **SuccessFactors dependant columns come from the related person.** The dependant sheets in the Employee workbook hold the related person's own data. The dictionary now names those fields, and the fill joins them from the related person instead of the employee.
- **Dictionary additions.** The SuccessFactors dictionary gains the dependant fields, the pay grade status and the frequency annualisation factor. Every name comes from the SuccessFactors data model; none is invented.
- **Added key columns follow the sheet's spelling.** When a sheet has no person key, Meridian adds one. On a sheet written in the import spelling (`first-name`), the key is now `person-id-external`, the header the import expects, and not the OData name `personIdExternal`.
- **More sheet types are recognised**, including the contingent worker sheet and the company alias.

The two unmatched Employee columns are on the Pay Component sheet, which mixes recurring and non-recurring pay rows. They need a person to fill them. The Foundation workbook ships its picklist sheet with no rows, so its picklist values are not checked until a picklist export is uploaded.

## Licence

The customer licence on the licence server now holds 12 modules: material master, customer master with business partner, and the ten SuccessFactors modules. The client picks up the change at its next licence check, and at once after this release restarts the services. No environment variable changes.
