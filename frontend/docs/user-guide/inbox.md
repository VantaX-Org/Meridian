# Inbox

The inbox is the steward's queue: every open task that needs a person's decision, most urgent first.

## What arrives here

Tasks come from findings assigned to you or your team, merge decisions, golden record reviews and proposals that need approval. Switch to **Exceptions** to see exceptions raised by exception rules on new findings.

## Working the queue

The tiles at the top count overdue tasks and tasks due today. Use the view buttons to look at your tasks, breached tasks or everything, and filter by type or search.

Select a row to open the record behind it. You can also act on several rows at once: select them, then approve, reject with a reason, escalate or assign them to yourself.

## Keyboard keys

The inbox is built for the keyboard:

- `j` and `k` move to the next or previous task.
- `Enter` opens the record.
- `a` assigns the task to you.
- `r` resolves, which approves it.
- `x` rejects it with a reason.
- `Escape` closes a panel.

Press `?` on any page to see every shortcut, grouped, with the inbox keys first when you are in the inbox.

## Service levels

Each task has a service level. At risk and breached tasks are marked so you can take them first. Leads and managers also see a team panel with workload and how often AI recommendations are accepted.

## Exception rules

If you may manage them, **Manage exception rules** lets you switch rules on or off and add new ones.
