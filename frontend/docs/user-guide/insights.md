# Insights

Insights turns the findings of a run into answers for people who decide: where to focus, what it costs, and who owns it.

## Readiness and impact

- **Readiness** shows which objects and processes are Go, At risk or No-go, so you can see what stops a migration wave.
- **Value at risk** shows money tied to defects. Proven cost is measured on posted documents that broke a rule, not estimated. It is shown per top-level process and never added across processes.

## Owners and trends

- **Owner scorecards** list each owner's score, open items by severity and whether they are improving. Start with the lowest score.
- **Trends** and **Forecast** show the score over runs and where it is heading.
- **Rule ranking** lists the rules costing the most.

## Reports

**Executive summary** gives the verdict, the trend and the blocking rules in one page, and exports as a PDF. **Report library** holds the board report and the analyst workbook.

## Process readiness and discovery

Process readiness shows each business process and the fields it needs. Process discovery goes further: it rebuilds the real flow from your documents. It shows:

- **Variants**, the distinct paths cases take. The most common is the happy path.
- **Conformance**, how closely cases follow the reference model, summarised as fitness from 0 to 1.
- **The designer**, where you edit the model and overlay the latest analysis to see which steps are blocked.

You can export a discovered model for Signavio.

## Other analyses

Lineage and impact traces where a field comes from and what depends on it. Pattern mining finds duplicates, anomalies and relationships across your data.
