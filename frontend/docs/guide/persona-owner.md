# Walkthrough: data owner

You are accountable for one or more objects and you are the second pair of eyes: you approve or send back the fix batches stewards prepare, sign off exceptions, approve contracts and glossary terms, and decide who covers for you when you are away. Your start screen is the **Landscape** with the line "2 batches wait for your approval, SLA met on 92% of your open work" and the next step "Approve batch B-241".

## Your first five steps

1. **Landscape**: read the line under the sentence and press **Approve batch B-241**.
2. **Batch inspector**: read the facts, the changes ("`MARA-MEINS` STK to EA") and the approval trail. **Approve** or **Send back** with a reason.
3. **Waiting for approval**: in Work, the chip lists every batch waiting, grouped by batch.
4. **Your object**: open an object you own; **Watch** it and apply the steward template to it.
5. **Out of office**: before you are away, set who covers your approvals.

## Your jobs and where they are done

- **Approve or send back a batch (four eyes)**: Batch inspector, **Approve**, **Send back**. You never approve a batch you built.
- **Out of office: approvals route to the deputy**: Preferences or People, **Out of office**; the batch and person inspectors show the cover.
- **Escalation chain after an SLA breach**: Work or People, **Triage and SLA**, **Escalation chain** (changing it needs manage triage).
- **Create and edit a data contract**: People, **Data contracts**, **New contract**; the contract inspector.
- **Approve a contract (never your own)**: Contract inspector.
- **Contract versions and revisions**: Contract inspector, **Versions**, **Revise as a new version**.
- **Contract breaches**: Contract inspector, **Breaches**.
- **Apply the steward template to your objects**: the object canvas, steward template.
- **Watch your objects; subscriptions**: Object canvas, **Watch**; Preferences, What you hear about.
- **Gate status of your objects' wave**: Landscape, Waves view.
- **Sign records off**: Work, **not an issue** or **accept risk** with a reason.
- **Merge pairs and approve glossary terms**: an object's Golden records and Glossary tabs.

## The numbers you approve

- **Records** and **Value at risk** on a batch are the records in it and their cost-model estimate.
- **SLA met** is the share of your open work resolved within the SLA days set under Triage and SLA.

## When something is wrong

- **Approve is missing**: you built the batch, or your role lacks approve.
- **Approved values do not appear in SAP**: approval does not touch SAP. The batch is exported and loaded by your team's process; the next run confirms each record.
- **Contact**: the steward named on the batch for its content, your administrator for access.
