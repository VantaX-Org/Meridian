# Walkthrough: programme lead

You run the data programme: you need to know how ready each system and object is for the next wave, what holds it back, who is working on it, and what to tell the steering committee. Your start screen is the **Landscape** with the next-step button for your nearest wave.

## Your first five steps

1. **Landscape**: read the go-live banner on top (the verdict for your next wave, what blocks it, days to go-live and the cost of waiting), then press the next step, for example "Clear the 3 no-go objects in Wave 1: finance foundations".
2. **Waves**: switch the landscape to **Waves**, open **Edit** on the wave and set its gate: min score, zero criticals, contracts met. **Save the gate**.
3. **An object**: open a no-go object and read its receipt: measured, capped, by which critical rules.
4. **People**: see who holds the open work and **Balance work** if one person holds most of it.
5. **Reports**: schedule the board pack for the steering committee.

## Your jobs and where they are done

- **Landscape readiness by system and wave**: Landscape, Coverage and Waves views.
- **Plan waves (objects, dates, stages)**: Migration waves panel, **Add a wave**, **Objects**.
- **Wave gate criteria**: Migration waves panel, **Gate**, **Save the gate**.
- **Gate status**: the Waves view, "Gate not passed, 2 of 3 not met".
- **Readiness certificate**: the waves panel shows the latest issued version and its state ("Version 2, waiting for signatures", "Version 2, signed" or "Not issued"); the certificate link opens it to issue a version, sign it or download its PDF.
- **Sign off a wave**: **Sign off** on the wave in the Migration waves panel (needs approve); only a wave whose verdict is go can be signed off, and any later change to the wave clears the sign-off.
- **Balance work between stewards**: People, **Balance work** (even counts, whole objects or by the assignment template), applied with undo.
- **Read data contracts and their breaches**: People, **Data contracts**.
- **Watch objects**: **Watch** on the object header for run, score and breach notices.
- **Export the ledger or any grid**: **Share and export**, **Export to Excel**.
- **Share a canvas with the steering committee**: **Share and export**, **Share this page as a link** (read-only, expires).
- **Start extraction and analysis, schedules**: System inspector; Administration, Start and schedule.
- **Reports and report schedules**: Reports.

## The numbers you report

- **The shown score** is capped: 85 at one critical rule over tolerance, 70 at two or more. Report the measured score beside it when it matters; the receipt states both.
- **Value at risk** is from the cost model named in the footer; it is an estimate from your own cost settings, not a forecast.
- **Go, at risk, no-go** come from the score bands under Administration, Settings.

## When something is wrong

- **The steering committee sees different numbers**: check which run each of you reads. The run is in the top bar and in every report.
- **A wave cannot pass its gate**: open each object named under **Zero criticals**; the critical rules over tolerance are listed in its receipt.
- **Contact**: SAP Basis for extraction failures, your administrator for access, the object owner for approvals that wait.
