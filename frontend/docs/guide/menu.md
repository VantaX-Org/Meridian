# The menu

The rail on the left is the menu. It has seven places: **Home**, **Objects**, **Runs**, **Fix**, **Rules**, **Master data** and **Migration**. Point at one, or move to it with the keyboard, and its menu opens beside the rail.

## What is on it

- **Home** (`/`) is the landscape: every system and object with its score.
- **Objects** (`/objects`) lists the systems and their objects as a tree, with a link to connect a system. See the Objects chapter.
- **Runs** (`/runs`) is the timeline of every measurement.
- **Fix** holds the Action centre, Work, verified fixes from Aletheia, Cleansing, Requests and People. A count on the link shows the open items.
- **Rules** holds Rules, rule promotion from Aletheia, the rule dry run, anomaly rules, learned monitors, verified survivorship from Aletheia and the evidence ledger.
- **Master data** holds MDM health, the entry gate, reference data, record links, Material 360, issues, data mining and process discovery.
- **Migration** holds Migration, S/4HANA readiness, go-live, the forecast and plan, value delivered, the score lens, cost and causes, and configuration. See the Migration chapter.
- **More**, below the rail, links to Reports, the Atlas and Administration.

Each menu has a **Pin the menu open** button and a **Close menu** button. On a narrow screen the rail becomes a bar at the bottom with the same seven places, and each opens a sheet.

## What you can do, by role

Everyone sees the same seven places on the rail. Administration, below them, shows only for people who can administer. What each person can do on the screens depends on the role, as the chapter for each screen says.

## The numbers and where they come from

The count on the Fix link comes from the open work the product holds. No figure on the rail is estimated.

## When something is wrong

- **A menu does not open**: point at the place again, or focus it with the keyboard. Selecting the place itself opens its main page.
- **The menu closes when you move away**: pin it open with **Pin the menu open**.

## Keyboard

- `g` then `h`, `o`, `r`, `f`, `u`, `m` or `i` goes to Home, Objects, Runs, Fix, Rules, Master data or Migration.
- `g` then `w` goes to Work, and `g` then `p` goes to People.
- `?` shows every shortcut.
